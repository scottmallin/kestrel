# Kestrel – Mapping

Lets venue operators draw maps of their premises and embed them, interactive and hosted by us, on their own websites.

## Structure

**Organisation**:
The paying customer that owns Maps and has members.
_Avoid_: Account, customer, tenant

**Plan**:
The subscription tier an Organisation is on, which sets its limits and the features it can use.
_Avoid_: Tier, package, subscription level

**Listing Manager**:
A member of an Organisation who can edit and publish Listings but cannot change Spaces or other geometry.
_Avoid_: Content editor, marketer

**Map**:
A single embeddable, publishable representation of a venue, made up of one or more Buildings and optionally an Outdoor Area.
_Avoid_: Project, site, venue

**Building**:
A structure within a Map, containing one or more Levels.
_Avoid_: Block, structure

**Level**:
One floor of a Building, on which Spaces are drawn. Its ordinal is its floor number: 0 for the ground floor, negative for basements.
_Avoid_: Floor, storey, story

**Outdoor Area**:
The ground-level area of a Map that lies between and around Buildings, treated as a Level that belongs to the Map instead of to any Building.
_Avoid_: Grounds, exterior, campus

**Space**:
A drawn, enclosed area on a Level with a permanent reference such as a unit number or room code.
_Avoid_: Room, unit, shape, polygon

**Space Reference**:
An optional, permanent code for a Space (such as "Unit 14" or "G.01"), unique within its Building.
_Avoid_: Room number, unit number, ID

**Space Kind**:
The type of a Space, chosen from a fixed list we define (such as unit, corridor, toilet, stairs, lift, entrance, void), which decides how it looks and how it can be used.
_Avoid_: Space type, room type

**Lettable**:
Describes a Space Kind (such as a unit, office or kiosk) whose Spaces are offered to occupants.

**Vacant**:
Describes a Lettable Space that has no current Listing. It's worked out from that, never set by hand.
_Avoid_: Empty, available, void

**Facility**:
A Space whose Space Kind Visitors look for directly (toilets, lifts, stairs, escalators, entrances), so it appears in the directory even without a Listing.
_Avoid_: Service, utility

**Amenity**:
A facility shown as an icon at a single point on a Level (such as an ATM, defibrillator or info desk), with no drawn area.
_Avoid_: Marker, pin, POI, icon

## Content

**Listing**:
The thing a visitor searches for (a shop, department or facility), with its name, logo, category and hours, linked to one or more Spaces.
_Avoid_: Tenant, occupant, POI, store

**Unplaced Listing**:
A Listing not yet linked to any Space. It exists in the Draft but is never shown in an Embed.
_Avoid_: Orphan, unlinked listing

**Category**:
A grouping of Listings defined by an Organisation (such as "Fashion" or "Physics Department"), used to organise the directory and search.
_Avoid_: Tag, type, sector

**Suggestion**:
A Space (with a proposed Space Kind and label) generated by AI from an Underlay, which a person must accept, adjust or discard before it becomes part of the Draft.
_Avoid_: AI result, prediction, auto-trace

**Underlay**:
An uploaded image or PDF of an existing plan, shown beneath a Level so its Spaces can be traced over it.
_Avoid_: Background, trace image, template

**Alignment**:
Fitting a Level's Underlay to its Building's shared plane, done by pinning two matching points to an already-aligned Level.
_Avoid_: Registration, calibration

## Publishing

**Draft**:
The editable working copy of a Map, made up of the Operations applied to it.
_Avoid_: Working copy, unsaved changes

**Operation**:
A single recorded change to a Draft, such as adding a Space, moving a corner or linking a Listing.
_Avoid_: Edit, change, action, command

**Published Version**:
An unchangeable snapshot of a Map's geometry and its Listings, taken from the Draft. Embeds always show the latest one. On some Plans, Listings can be republished without republishing geometry.
_Avoid_: Release, live map, revision

**Embed**:
A configured way of showing a Map on a customer's website or screen, with its own starting view, mode, theme and Allowed Domains. It always shows the Map's latest Published Version. A Map can have several.
_Avoid_: Widget, plugin, iframe

**Kiosk Mode**:
An Embed mode for fixed in-venue touchscreens, with a permanent You Are Here and controls that reset after inactivity.
_Avoid_: Signage mode, totem

**You Are Here**:
A fixed position on a Level that an Embed treats as the Visitor's location.
_Avoid_: Current location, origin

**Allowed Domain**:
A website domain an Embed is permitted to load on.
_Avoid_: Whitelist, referrer

## Insights

**Visitor**:
An anonymous member of the public using an Embed.
_Avoid_: User, end user, customer

**Search Event**:
A record that a Visitor searched for something or viewed a Listing in an Embed.
_Avoid_: Hit, analytics event, impression
